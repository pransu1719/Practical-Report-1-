## 🎬 Plot Twists (Key Insights)

<details>
<summary>👑 <b>Twist #1: The close race in categories</b></summary>

Technology wins, but Furniture and Office Supplies are only a whisker behind. No category is carrying the business alone, which means a **healthy, balanced portfolio**.
</details>

<details>
<summary>🌴 <b>Twist #2: West is a superstar</b></summary>

The West region brings in **more than 3x** the value of Central. Whatever the West is doing right, the other regions should copy it.
</details>

<details>
<summary>🏷️ <b>Twist #3: The discount dilemma</b></summary>

With a **16% average discount**, profit lands at about **12% of sales**. Discounts bring customers in, but they quietly eat into margins. Worth a closer look!
</details>

<details>
<summary>🏜️ <b>Twist #4: Central has untapped potential</b></summary>

The lowest region today, and the biggest opportunity for tomorrow.
</details>
